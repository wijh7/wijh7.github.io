Breadlight Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-11
App: Breadlight (Android), package com.breadlight.app
Developer: Hendry Wijaya
Contact: support.wijh7@gmail.com

Breadlight is a Bible reader, guided quiet-time companion and personal spiritual journal. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, and what choices you have. We have written it to describe how the app actually works, in plain language.

1. The short version

  • Breadlight has no ads, no analytics and no user accounts.
  • Your journal, prayers, reflections, notes, bookmarks, highlights, reading position and quiet-time answers are stored only on your device. Breadlight does not send them to us or to anyone else.
  • Purchases of Breadlight Pro are handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details.
  • Breadlight turns off Android's automatic cloud backup for its data. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, your writing is deleted. You can export your journal to a file at any time.

2. Information stored on your device

Breadlight saves the following in the app's private storage on your device:

What Why
Journal entries (date, entry type, title, text, optional linked Bible verse) So you can keep and reread your journal
Notes, bookmarks and highlights on Bible verses So your annotations appear when you read
Quiet-time session answers and progress So you can leave a session and continue later
Reading position and recent reading history So the Bible opens where you left off
Settings (text size, spacing, theme, translation, interface language) To remember your preferences
Your current Breadlight Pro status and when Google Play last confirmed it So Pro keeps working when you are offline
Installed Bible text So you can read without a connection
Which Daily Scripture verse you were shown today So the verse stays the same all day
A Scripture Art image you chose to share, kept temporarily in the app's cache and replaced the next time you share So the app you share to can read it

This information stays on your device. We, the developer, have no server that receives it and no way to read it.

Other apps cannot read Breadlight's private storage under Android's normal app-sandbox protections. Breadlight does not add its own encryption on top of this; if your device has encryption and a screen lock enabled, those protections also apply. Anyone who can unlock your device and open Breadlight can read your journal.

3. Information Breadlight sends or receives over the network

Breadlight itself does not make network requests. It does not contact any developer-run server and contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software development kits (SDKs).

The only network-related activity is through Google Play Billing, a library provided by Google that talks to the Google Play Store app on your device in order to:

  • show you Breadlight Pro prices for your country;
  • process a purchase you choose to make;
  • tell Breadlight which Breadlight Pro products your Google account owns (for example when you open the app or tap Restore purchases).

From Google Play, Breadlight receives purchase information such as the product purchased, purchase state, purchase time, whether a subscription renews automatically, and a purchase token. Breadlight uses this only to decide whether Pro is active and to acknowledge purchases as Google requires. It stores only the resulting Pro status and the time it was confirmed; it does not send purchase information to us.

Google processes your payment and account information under Google's own terms and privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Breadlight never sees your card or bank details.

4. Information we, the developer, may receive

  • From Google Play Console: Google gives developers sales reports (for example, the number of purchases and revenue by country) and app-quality reports such as crash and "app not responding" statistics, when you have allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics with Google. These are produced and processed by Google, not by code in Breadlight.
  • If you email us: we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply and to help you, and we do not add you to any mailing list.

We do not sell personal information, share it for advertising, or use it to build a profile of you.

5. Things you choose to share

  • Export journal: creates a file containing your journal entries at a location you choose. Once exported, the file is under your control and is handled by whatever app or service you saved it to.
  • Copy verse: places the verse text on your device clipboard, where other apps you use may read it.
  • Scripture Art: when you tap Share, the image is offered to the app you pick in Android's share menu, which can then read it; when you tap Save image, it is saved to your Pictures/Breadlight folder (Android 10 and later) or to a location you choose. The image contains only the Bible text, its reference and translation, and optionally the word "BREADLIGHT". It never includes your journal or notes.
  • Share a verse (Morning Card, seasonal card, A Verse for Someone): when you tap Share, Breadlight composes plain text and offers it to the app you pick in Android's share menu, which can then read it. The text contains the exact verse, its reference and translation, a short line written by Breadlight or by you, the name you typed (if any) and a link to Breadlight on Google Play. Nothing you type in these fields is stored by Breadlight or sent to us. It never includes your journal or notes.
  • Export journal as PDF (Pro): creates a PDF of your journal at a location you choose, using your own writing and only the reference of any linked verse. Once saved, the file is under your control.
  • Manage subscription: opens Google Play's subscription page.

6. Permissions

Breadlight does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files. Saving a Scripture Art image uses Android's photo library or file picker, which need no permission and do not give Breadlight access to your other photos or files. The Google Play Billing library adds Android's billing permission so purchases can work.

7. Keeping and deleting your information

  • You can edit or delete any journal entry, note, bookmark or highlight inside the app at any time.
  • Settings › Delete all my data removes your journal, notes, highlights, bookmarks, quiet-time progress and reading place from the device in one step. It cannot be undone, so export your journal first if you want a copy.
  • Settings › Import journal restores a file you exported, for example on a new phone. The file never leaves your device.
  • Uninstalling Breadlight, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, permanently deletes everything Breadlight stored on the device. We cannot recover it, because we never had a copy.
  • Losing or ending Breadlight Pro never deletes your writing. If you have more entries than the Free plan allows, they all stay readable, editable and deletable.
  • Your purchase records are kept by Google. To request deletion of data held by Google, use your Google account settings.
  • Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to help you, and you can ask us to delete them.

8. Children

Breadlight is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. Breadlight does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, beyond what is described above. If you believe a child has emailed us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under California law in the United States or Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law, UU No. 27 of 2022), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your personal data, or to object to certain processing. Because Breadlight keeps your app data only on your device, you can exercise most of these directly in the app: read, edit, delete or export your entries. For anything else, including information in emails you sent us, contact support.wijh7@gmail.com. We do not sell or "share" personal information as those terms are used in California law.

10. Changes to this policy

If Breadlight starts handling information differently (for example, if an optional cloud backup is added in the future), we will update this policy before the change takes effect, change the effective date, and, where the change is significant, tell you inside the app. Any future feature that would send your writing off your device will be optional and off by default.
