About Noya and its limits

  • Noya is a personal tracking and wellbeing tool. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a doctor about your health.
  • Predictions are estimates, not guarantees. Do not rely on them to prevent or plan a pregnancy.
  • Noya does not give religious guidance. Ask a qualified scholar or religious leader about personal religious questions.
  • The chat is an automated, supportive tool. It is not a therapist or an emergency service. In an emergency, contact your local emergency number.
  • Some translations are marked beta and may contain mistakes.

Plans

  • Free stays free: tracking, predictions, daily log, calendar, faith cards, Partner Mode and 10 Companion chats a day. One small promo card a day may be shown; you can skip it by sharing Noya, or remove it with Pro.
  • Pro (yearly) is a Google Play subscription that renews every year until you cancel it in Play Store > Payments & subscriptions. Cancelling keeps Pro until the end of the paid year.
  • Lifetime is a one-time Google Play purchase that unlocks everything in Pro for as long as Noya offers the app, including features added later.
  • Free trial: 7 days of Pro features on your device, with no payment and no account. When it ends, the app returns to Free; your data is kept.
  • Refunds follow Google Play's refund policies. Prices are shown in Google Play before you confirm and may differ by country.
  • Partner Mode is an information-sharing feature fully controlled by the account owner. Each link shows only what the account owner chose to share for that day and ends after that day. Noya is not responsible for use outside the app.
