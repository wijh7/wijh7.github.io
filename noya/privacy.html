Noya Privacy Policy

App: Noya - Cycle Tracker (package app.noya.cycle)
Effective date: 11 November 2026
Contact: support.wijh7@gmail.com

In short: your cycle and health information stays on your phone. Noya has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer does not collect or receive your cycle data. The free version of Noya shows ads: one small, static ad card a day, with no ad network, no advertising ID and no tracking.

1. Who we are

Noya is a cycle-tracking and wellbeing app made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). You can reach us at support.wijh7@gmail.com.

2. Information the app handles

You choose what to enter. Depending on how you use Noya, this can include: your age range, goal, optional religion setting and cycle type from onboarding; daily logs (bleeding, pain, mood, sleep, stress, discharge); notes you write; a personal fasting counter; settings; and the profile names you create.

All of this is stored only on your device, in a database encrypted with AES-256-GCM. By default the encryption key is held by your device. You can add your own password for extra protection.

3. What we collect

We do not collect your personal or health data. Noya has no sign-up, no login, no developer server that receives your data, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, no advertising or ad-network SDK and no tracking. Because we do not hold your data, we cannot read it, sell it, share it, or hand it over.

4. Things that leave your phone only when you choose

  • Sharing: when you tap a share button (for example a Cycle Story image, a PDF for your doctor, or an invitation to a friend), your phone's share sheet opens and you choose where it goes. Noya does not send anything by itself. Once you share a file or message, the receiving app or person handles it under their own terms.
  • Partner link: if you create one, the link contains only what you chose to share for that day: the cycle phase in plain words (for example, "period days"), optionally your mood, symptom chips you pick, and, only if you switch it on, an expected range for your next period. It never contains your calendar, history, notes or fertility estimates, it is not sent to or stored on any server, and it stops working after that day. You decide who receives it.
  • Backup file: if you export a backup, it is saved as a file you choose, encrypted with a backup password you type. Noya does not upload it. If you forget that password, the backup cannot be opened by anyone, including us.
  • External links: the support link in the chat opens findahelpline.com in your browser. That website has its own privacy policy.

5. Purchases

Optional paid plans are bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy. Noya only learns whether a purchase is active on your Google account; we never see your card or bank details. The free trial runs on your device and needs no payment or account.

6. Ads

Noya contains ads. Free users may see one small ad card per day under the calendar. It is either a Noya card (for example about Noya Pro, or inviting a friend) or a card for a product from a curated partner brand in a limited set of categories: sanitary pads, skincare, vitamins and herbal tea. These ads are plain static content shipped with the app or refreshed from a static Noya file. They are not served by a third-party advertising network or SDK, use no advertising ID, and are not personalised: the app does not send your cycle data, profile or any identifier to choose or show a card. If a card has a link, it opens in your browser only when you tap it, and that website has its own privacy policy. We never show promotional cards to Noya Pro or Lifetime users, in Teen Mode, or in Discreet Mode. We do not allow ads for gambling, loans, alcohol, tobacco or pregnancy tests. You can hide the card for the day by sharing Noya with a friend, or remove all ads with Noya Pro.

7. Permissions

Noya works without account access, location, contacts, camera or microphone. Any file or share action uses the standard Android file picker or share sheet that you control. If you turn on reminders, Noya asks for Android notification permission. Reminders are scheduled on your own device, with no server involved, and their wording is neutral while Discreet Mode is on. You can turn them off in Settings or in your phone settings at any time.

8. Children

Noya is not directed at children under 13 as an audience for any online service, and it does not collect children's data. There are no accounts, and we never ask for a name, photo, school or email. Everything is stored locally on the device. Teen Mode uses gentler wording and turns off partner features. If you are under 18, we encourage you to use Noya together with a parent or trusted adult.

9. Your choices and deleting your data

  • See what is stored: Settings shows what Noya keeps on your phone.
  • Delete My Data / Panic Wipe: Settings can erase everything. It overwrites the stored database, then deletes it and its encryption key. We do not promise physical erasure from your phone's flash storage, but the key is destroyed and the remaining data cannot be read.
  • Uninstalling the app also removes its data from your device.
  • Because we do not hold your data, there is nothing on our side to access, correct or delete. If you lose your password or delete your data, we cannot recover it.

10. Security

We use encryption on your device and keep no copy elsewhere. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your phone locked and your passwords private. Discreet Mode (the calculator screen) is a disguise for privacy in front of other people, not strong security by itself.

11. Not medical advice

Noya is a personal tracking and wellbeing tool. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Predictions are estimates, not guarantees. Please talk to a doctor about your health. The companion chat is an automated tool, not a therapist or emergency service; in an emergency contact your local emergency number.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how Noya handles information, we will update this page and its effective date, and the app will link to the current version.
